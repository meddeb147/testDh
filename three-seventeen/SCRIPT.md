# 3:17 AM — Voice-over script

If you ever get a text from your own number at three seventeen in the morning… do exactly what it says.

Last night, my phone lit up. Three seventeen. The sender… was me. One message: "Don't go downstairs."

I figured it was a glitch. A prank. I almost went back to sleep. Then I heard it. The fridge door. Downstairs. Opening… and closing.

My phone buzzed again: "It's in the kitchen. Stay in bed." Then: "Don't answer when Mom calls you."

And right then… from the bottom of the stairs… my mom's voice. Soft. Sweet. "Honey? Come down here a second."

I swear, I almost went. My foot was already on the floor. Then my phone buzzed one last time. But this text wasn't from me. It was from Mom.

"Are you awake? Don't make a sound. I'm hiding in my closet. Someone's in the kitchen."

I didn't move until sunrise. When I finally went downstairs, the kitchen was empty. Every cabinet… wide open. Every chair… turned to face the stairs.

I still don't know who sent those texts. But whatever was in that kitchen can copy any voice it hears. It just can't text.

So if you hear your name tonight… check your phone before you answer.

Send this to someone who sleeps with their phone by the bed. And follow for part two.

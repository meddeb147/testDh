If you ever get a text message from your own number at three seventeen in the morning… do exactly what it says. Last night, my phone lit up. Three seventeen a.m. The sender… was me. One message: “Don't go downstairs.” I thought it was a glitch. Maybe a prank. I almost went back to sleep. And I heard something. Fridge door. Downstairs. Opening… and closing. My phone buzzed again: “It's in the kitchen. Stay in bed.” Another message: “Don't answer when Mom calls you.” And right then… from the bottom of the stairs… I heard my mom's voice. “Honey? Come down here a second.” I swear, I almost went. My foot was already on the floor. Then my phone buzzed one last time. This time… the message wasn't from me. It was from Mom. “Are you awake? Don't make a sound. I'm hiding in my closet. Someone's in the kitchen.” I froze. I didn't move. I didn't even breathe. I stayed in that bed until sunrise. When I finally went downstairs, the kitchen was empty. Every cabinet… wide open. Every chair… turned to face the stairs. I still don't know who sent those first messages. But I know one thing. Whatever was in that kitchen can copy any voice it hears. It just can't text. So if you hear someone calling your name tonight… don't answer. Check your phone first.
